## 2026-11-26 (MILESTONE 3): DRIVER, KERNEL PATCH & BINARY REWRITER OPTIMIZATIONS FOR iPadOS 26.1
- **Userland Metal BO Recycle Pool (`hakios-metal/include/hakios-metal/gpu.hpp`, `hakios-metal/src/gpu.cpp`, `hakios-metal/src/iokit_transport.cpp`)**:
  - Added a thread-safe, fence-aware GEM Buffer Object Recycle Pool (`<= 64` BOs, `<= 8 MiB`/BO, `<= 32 MiB` cap) on IOKit `KernelGpu`, reducing transient Metal buffer allocation from 5 Mach traps + 2 `vm_map` mutations to **0 syscalls** once `completedFence_` passes `lastFence_` (with `MSM_PREP_NOSYNC` fallback, `ENOMEM` auto-drain, and exact `allocatedBytes()` accounting).
- **GPU & 120Hz Display Optimizations (`xnu/patches-ipados26/zz-0102-ipados26-gpu-display-opt.patch`)**:
  - `SapphireGpu.cpp`: Persistent 1 GiB WC `gpool_kva` mapping (zero per-BO `pmap`/TLB shootdowns), `MSM_BO_NOZERO`, stack submit arrays (`stack_bos[64]`, `stack_cmds[16]`), `fLeftUsed` $O(1)$ fast-exit + single batched SMMU `tlb_flush()`, `fNextSlot` handle hints, and 64-bit word `bits_mark` + `__builtin_ctzll` VA allocation.
  - `SweetFramebuffer.cpp`: Eliminated the `19.4M dc civac/sec` stall on `sweetfb_native=1` Write-Combined flip buffers, added a `-mgeneral-regs-only`-safe 64-bit SWAR premultiplied `BGRA8888` multi-layer alpha compositor + full-frame contiguous `memcpy`, and wired `RequestPowerChange` to `sweet_cpufreq_panel_notify()`.
- **Dynamic OSM CPUFreq, `TSENS0` Thermal, UFS & 120Hz Touch (`xnu/patches-ipados26/zz-0103-ipados26-cpufreq-tsens-ufs-touch.patch`)**:
  - `sweet_cpufreq.c`: Full dynamic Qualcomm OSM DVFS (`0x0f521000`/`0x0f523000`) + live `TSENS0` (`0x04410000`/`0x04411000`) silicon temperature sysctls (`kern.sweet_tsens`, `kern.sweet_tsens_sensors`, `kern.sweet_cpu_max_freq`, `kern.sweet_gpu_max_freq`, `kern.sweet_touch_boost_enabled`, `kern.sweet_launch_boost_enabled`, `kern.sweet_thermal_zone0..3`, `kern.sweet_thermal_state`, `kern.sweet_thermal_policy`, `kern.sweet_deep_sleep`) matched to `userland/thermal/thermald.c`.
  - `sweet_ufs.c`: Enabled `ufscache=1` and `ufswait=1` (`50 us` spin + `100 us` sleep yield) by default.
  - `sweet_touch.c`: Added 21-byte fast FocalTech I2C reads (`0.51 ms` vs `1.50 ms` at 400 kHz when `<= 2` fingers active) + touch-down CPU boost.
- **ARMv8.2 `FEAT_FP16` / `FEAT_RDM` Emulator & Offline Rewriter (`xnu/patches-ipados26/zz-0104-ipados26-armv82-fp16-rdm-emu.patch`, `tools/ipados26_depac.py`, `tools/ipados26-root-tweaks.py`)**:
